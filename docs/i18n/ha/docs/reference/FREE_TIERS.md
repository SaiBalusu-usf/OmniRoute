## Sabuntawar 2026-06-17 — abin da ya canza tun daga 2026-06-05

An sabunta dukan kundin bayan wani binciken yanar gizo na wakilai 50 (takardun hukuma + labaran kwanaki 7 na baya-bayan nan, waɗanda aka tabbatar ta hanyar gwajin saɓani). Muhimman bayanai:

- **An cire / babu matakin kyauta (2026):** `chutes` (matakin kyauta ya ƙare 2026-03), `phind` (kamfani ya rufe 2026-01), `kluster` (an dakatar 2026-06-09 → MITO), `gitlawb` + `gitlawb-gmi` (an soke kyautar MiMo 2026-05-24, tallan Nemotron ya ƙare 2026-06 — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `aimlapi` (an dakatar da matakin kyauta na ɗan lokaci — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `yi` (an yi ritayar Yi-Light, biyan gwargwadon amfani — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `featherless-ai` (babu matakin kyauta a halin yanzu). `iflytek` / `sparkdesk` suna nan a jeri amma suna ɗauke da bayanin taka-tsantsan kan ToS (Spark Lite kyauta ne; ToS ɗin yana ƙuntata amfani da proxy/relay).
- **Gemini** — an rufe `2.0 Flash` / `2.0 Flash-Lite` a 2026-06-01 kuma `2.5 Pro` ya fita daga matakin kyauta (2026-04); yanzu matakin kyauta ya ƙunshi **iyalan Flash kawai** (2.5/3/3.1/3.5 Flash + Gemma). Yanzu kundin yana **haɗa** iyalan Flash a matsayin rukuni guda (a baya an kumbura ƙididdigar ta hanyar ƙirga kowane nau'i dabam: 462M → 60M).
- **Lambobin da aka gyara:** `cloudflare-ai` 122M → **30M** (ainihin Neurons 10k/rana), an sake rarraba `doubao` a matsayin ƙimar rajista ta lokaci guda (ba mai maimaituwa ba), `llm7` 4M → **150M** (tokens 5M/rana kamar yadda takardu suka nuna), an dakatar da endpoints na `together` masu ƙarewa da "-Free" → an kuma cire ƙimar rajista ta $25 (ana buƙatar mafi ƙarancin sayen $5, babu gwajin kyauta), lokacin Preview na `longcat` ya ƙare + an yi ritayar samfuran Flash → **LongCat-2.0** kawai, an sake rarraba shi a matsayin ƙimar rajista ta lokaci guda ta tokens **10M** (ana buƙatar KYC, ba mai maimaituwa ba).
- **Sabbin masu samar da sabis na kyauta da aka gano:** ⭐ **Kilo Code** (`kilo-gateway` — jerin "Auto Free" mai juyawa: iyalan NVIDIA Nemotron 3, StepFun, Poolside, Nex-N2-Pro), ⭐ **OpenCode Zen** (`opencode-zen` — samfuran coding na kyauta 6 masu juyawa), ⭐ **Z.AI / Zhipu** (`glm-cn` — GLM-4-Flash / 4.5-Flash / 4.7-Flash kyauta na dindindin + kari na rajista 20M), da `arcee-ai` Trinity Large Preview.
- **Sabbin matakai na gaskiya** (duba Hanyar Aiki): nau'in _kyauta-na-dindindin-amma-ba-shinge_ (ainihin damar shiga mai maimaituwa, babu iyakar token da za a ƙirga) da _ƙarin da ajiya ke buɗewa_ (OpenRouter $10 → +24M/wata), duka an nuna su **dabam-dabam** domin kada su taɓa kumbura babban adadin da aka bayyana.

> Cikakken teburin kowane mai samarwa da ke ƙasa hoton yanayin **2026-06-05** ne; sauye-sauyen da ke sama sun maye gurbinsa. Tushen kai-tsaye kuma na hukuma shi ne kundin kowane samfur `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`.

## Tsaruka biyu — ƙirgawa da yanke shawara

OmniRoute yana amsa "kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsaruka biyu waɗanda da gangan suke karanta
mabambantan tushe:

| Tsari                | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                                                    | Inda ake nunawa                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ƙirgawa / nunawa** | Kundin da aka warware — tushen da aka tura wanda bayanan Radar suka lulluɓe (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                                                   | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafi, allunan bayanai                                                                          |
| **Yanke shawara**    | Kundin da aka tura kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a cikin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da dabarun tantancewa na cikin gida (`:free` suffix, farashin sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Duk mai amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfur, routing na `auto/*`, `GET /v1/models`, da samfotin burauza |

Ƙirgawa na iya inganta duk lokacin da ake samun bayanan feed. Yanke shawara yana nan kan
artifact ɗin release, don haka amsar tana zama iri ɗaya a burauza da kan uwar garke,
ana iya maimaita ta ba tare da intanet ba, kuma ana iya gwada ta ba tare da bayanan ma'ajiyar bayanai ba. Barin samfotin burauza
ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da bayanan uwar garke ke karanta wani zai samar da
samfoti da bai dace da abin da ke faruwa bayan dannawa ba — an kiyaye wannan rabuwar da
gangan.

## Hanyar aiki & abubuwan lura

- Alƙaluman **ƙiyasin iyaka mafi girma** ne daga iyakokin matakin kyauta da kowane mai samarwa ya rubuta zuwa **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna canzawa a kai a kai — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da wani adadi.
- **Abin da kowace shigarwa take tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu wata shigarwa da ke da ƙimar amincewa ta kowane layi, kuma API ɗin ma ba ya bayar da ita — ku ɗauki kowane adadin da ke sama a matsayin ƙiyasi mai inganci iri ɗaya da ba a bayyana ba. Bayanai guda biyu sun bambanta, domin ana tsara su da hannu maimakon fitar da su ta hasashe: shigarwa 5 suna da tabbatacciyar iyakar dakatarwa da aka rubuta daga tushe mai zaman kansa, kuma shigarwa 13 suna da bayanin bayyana horarwa da prompt. Ana saita `hardStopGuaranteed` ne kawai idan sharuddan mai samarwar da kansa sun ce wuce adadin kyauta zai sa a ƙi buƙatar maimakon a fara cajin ku a ɓoye, tare da tushen a cikin tsokaci kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ta tsohuwa, kuma shigarwar da babu wanda ya tabbatar da ita tana kasancewa ba a saita ta ba. Saboda haka, rashin alamar dakatarwa mai ƙarfi yana nufin "ba a tabbatar ba", ba wai "an san za a caje ku" ba. Yanayin **STRICT** (kariyar zaɓin shiga ta hanyar `freeAccessPolicy=strict`) yana amincewa ne kawai da shigarwar da ke ɗauke da alamar; ana cire duk wani matakin kyauta a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`).
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = tokens na wata-wata masu maimaituwa kawai. **Kuɗaɗen rajista na sau ɗaya ba sa maimaituwa** kuma ana ƙirga su a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar su ma 0 ne.
- Iyakar token ta yau da kullum → `monthly = daily × 30`. Idan RPD kaɗai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. Idan RPM/TPM kaɗai ne (babu iyakar yau da kullum) → **babu iyaka** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta ta dindindin, amma babu iyakar token da aka wallafa** (`siliconflow`, `glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan ainihin damar kyauta ce mai maimaituwa, amma ana iyakance ta da ƙimar amfani/yawan ayyukan lokaci guda. Muna rarraba su a matsayin `recurring-uncapped` kuma **ba ma taɓa tara su** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da iyakar ƙarya (irin kumburin da muka ƙi). An jera su ne domin ku san suna nan.
- **Ƙarin da ajiya ke buɗewa:** ƙaramin ƙarin kuɗi na sau ɗaya wanda ke ɗaga adadin kyauta har abada (OpenRouter: $10 → buƙatu 1000/rana ≈ +24M/wata). Ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin adadi na daban, kuma ba a haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin.
- **Adadin da cancanta ke iyakancewa** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): ainihin adadi ne mai maimaituwa wanda ake buɗewa kawai bayan tantance shaidar zama ta yankin (a yau, tabbatar da suna na ainihi a babban yankin China). Ana ƙirga shi ta amfani da ƙa’idar cire maimaituwar rukuni iri ɗaya zuwa wani adadi na daban (`gatedRecurringTokens`), kuma ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, don haka hanyar turawa ba ta canza ba.
- **Rukunonin hujja.** Ƙa’idar ita ce: adadi a cikin kundin yana ambaton tushensa a cikin tsokacin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai samarwa da kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (endpoint na mai samarwa da baya buƙatar tantancewa, misali endpoint na tsare-tsaren jama’a na NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (ana ganin adadin ne kawai a cikin console na asusu; tsokacin yana rubuta wanda ya gan shi da lokacin da ya gani, da kuma shafin jama’a da ke cewa iyakar tana nan). Halin da ake ciki yau: tubalan guda biyar da aka sake bincika a 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwar da suka gabaci sake binciken 2026-09-02 suna gadon binciken da ya gabata har sai an taɓa su; duk wani adadi **sabo ko wanda aka canza** ba tare da tsokacin hujja ba kuskure ne. A yau `mistral` kaɗai ne aka tabbatar ta console.

## Me ya sa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tara bayanai ƙanƙanta

Yawancin alkaluman "token kyauta a kowane wata" a wannan fanni jimillar adadin da aka ware wa kowane samfuri ne. Namu ba haka yake ba, da gangan:

- **Ana ƙirga kowace ma'ajiyar bai ɗaya sau ɗaya kawai.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral yana da kaso guda na 1B/wata ga kowace ƙungiya; jera shi ƙarƙashin samfura biyar ba ya mai da shi 5B. Idan aka tara bisa kowane samfuri, kundin namu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafawa a 2026-09-03; CI ba ya tabbatar da wannan adadi — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da kundin ya canza) — babban taken yana cewa **~1.47B** domin wannan ne ainihin abin da asusu ɗaya na kowane mai samarwa zai iya kashewa.
- **Ana sauya iyakokin yau da kullum, amma ba ƙimar gudu ba.** Iyakacin tokens/rana da aka rubuta ya zama `× 30`; iyakacin buƙatu/rana da aka rubuta ya zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; mai samarwa da yake wallafa buƙatu-a-kowane-minti kawai ba shi da adadin wata-wata kuma ana jera shi a matsayin _mara iyaka_, ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin jimilla ba. Ninka iyakar ƙimar gudu da 24/7 shi ne ƙarin ƙarya da muka ƙi yi.
- **Ana nuna ƙason da ke bayan tantance shaidar yanki dabam** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), domin yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da shi ba.
- **Kireditin rajista na watan farko ne kawai** kuma ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗa shi da adadin dindindin ba.
- **CI ne ke tilasta daidaiton adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimillar daga kundin kuma yana hana ginin kammalawa idan wannan fayil, README ko katin kasafin kuɗi suka kauce daga gare su.

## Teburin lura da ToS

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce, ba shingen zaɓin hanya ba.** Har yanzu ana haɗa masu samarwa da aka yi wa alamar `tos` cikin zaɓin hanya da haɗuwa/madadin ta asali; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Ma'aunin tambaya na `excludeTosAvoid` yana shafar kallon taƙaitawa ne kawai, ba zaɓin hanya na gaba ɗaya ba. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (na bayani ne, injunan zaɓin hanya ba sa karanta shi).

> Taƙaitaccen bayani kan sharuɗɗan kowane mai samarwa game da proxy na mutum ɗaya, mai masaukin kansa. `caution` = akwai wani sharadin amfani na mutum ko proxy da ya kamata a duba; `ambiguous` = ba a fayyace ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a sarari. Don bayani ne, ba shawarar doka ba — kai ne za ka yanke shawara.
